replacement (every site the string occurs at), the named targets rebuilt (Development build, GCC 13,
RTX 5060, Vulkan), a shader mutation's two committed headers regenerated by regenerate.py first, the
named suites run, and the file — and for a shader the two headers — restored and checked against its
md5 before the next. A mutation that did not compile was rewritten until it did and run again; only
compiled mutations are listed.

- M17 first survived smoke.gi_gpu_live, whose cases hold the views to the host's state rather than the
  gather's having run; smoke.gi_gpu_streaming now requires the gather timed in most frames and the
  probes it gathered valid, and kills it.
