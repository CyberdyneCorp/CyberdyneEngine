Mutations that turn integration.render_lightmap_bake red, Development build, macOS (Apple M2 Max,
Apple clang, Xcode 27.0). Everything in this change is host-side, so every claim is proved on the
host. Each mutation was applied, the suite rebuilt and run, the file restored and md5-verified.
Listed under each: the cases that failed. "skipped" counts cases a failed REQUIRE's abort left
unrun (the harness compiles doctest without exceptions, so a REQUIRE ends the process).

m04 SURVIVED its first run, when the case sampled the mask at the blocker chart's corner: the
conservative rasteriser covers the texel ring around a chart, so a base-level tap never reached a
texel the dilation fills. The case now checks every texel of the blocker's rectangle, which is what a
coarser mip level averages, and m04 is red.

THE TEXEL PLANES ARE UNCHANGED FOR A BAKE THAT SETS NO MOBILITY. Proved separately: an FNV-1a hash of
every texel plane of the ground-truth room, baked in each mode, was taken with main's
src/ stashed and again with this change applied. Identical:

    PLANES irradiance 16384 8afe058e6bcf2383
    PLANES directional 32768 7fe4d819439ba383
    PLANES sh-l1 49152 e62fed5fcc984383
