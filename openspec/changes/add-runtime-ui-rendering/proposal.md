# Draw CyberUI on the device, with a developer console and a strategy HUD on it

## Why

`src/ui` is a tested retained-mode core — store, layout, `.cyss`, interaction, flattening, budget,
accessibility — and nothing submits its output. `ui-system`'s "Rendering" and "GPU-driven rendering"
requirements were answered as rectangles on the CPU, and its "Forcing functions" requirement warns
that a UI system with no demanding first-party consumer decays: the tree had one consumer, a sample
that copied primitive bounds into a text dump. This is stage 1 of issue #91: a renderer for the
primitive stream, and the first consumers on it.

## What changes

- `cy::ui` (`paint.h`): `Primitive` carries the built-in shape material's corner radius, border width
  and border colour; `BuiltinMaterial` names the one shader's behaviours (shape, image, glyph);
  `flatten()` multiplies opacity down the tree into premultiplied colours (`scale_premultiplied`),
  skips elements with nothing to draw (`FlattenReport::empty`), and takes an optional
  `ContentPainter` that paints an element's content — its glyphs — after its background and before
  its children, under its clip.
- `cy::ui-text` (`src/ui/text/`): `TextPainter`, a `ContentPainter` and `ContentMeasurer` over
  `TextServer`, and a built-in public-domain bitmap font (X11 `misc-fixed` 6x13, printable ASCII) as
  a temporary stand-in until real fonts are imported (#86). The atlas is warmed at start, so painting
  never grows it mid-frame.
- `cy::ui-render` (`src/ui/render/`): `encode.h` — the 64-byte row the shader reads, the scissor rule,
  one draw per batch, and the shader transcribed on the host (`shade_reference`, `draw_reference`);
  `ui_renderer.h` — `UiRenderer`, one graph pass at the frame's `UiAndDebug` stage, one
  `draw_indexed_indirect` per batch with its clip as a scissor and its atlas page bound, premultiplied
  `One, OneMinusSourceAlpha` blending over the tonemapped output. `shaders/ui.slang` is one shader
  with material-indexed behaviour: a signed-distance rounded box with a border, an image, a glyph.
  Committed SPIR-V and MSL, regenerated by `shaders/regenerate.py`.
- `cy::ui-console` (`src/ui/console/`): `DevConsole`, the developer console — commands, echo,
  `help`/`clear`/`echo`, a scrollback ring whose rows are virtualised (the store holds only the rows
  shown).
- The frame: `FrameDescription::ui_stage` and `FrameSinks::ui`, a `FrameStageDeclaration` for the
  `UiAndDebug` stage, handed the colour the post chain ended in. Without a producer the stage is the
  single pass it always was.
- `samples/13-rts-selection`: a strategy HUD in game code (`hud.h`) — resource bar, minimap with the
  camera's rectangle, a panel listing the selected units — and the console, drawn over the selection
  frame; `docs/design/images/rts-hud*.png`.
- Tests: `unit.ui` (opacity, content painting, empty containers, shape fields), `unit.ui_text`,
  `unit.ui_console`, `unit.ui_render`, `unit.render_forward` (the stage's producer), and
  `render.ui` (seven cases on a Vulkan device, one of them on an sRGB output), each proven red by
  a mutation.

## Scope

Stage 1 only. Not built: offscreen opacity groups (overlapping children of a faded panel each blend
on their own), the HDR composite before tonemapping, custom UI materials, blur-behind, world-space
and surface-space documents, the transform array (only the identity), multi-line text, and Swift
access to the interface. Metal's MSL is generated and committed and not exercised on this host;
D3D12 is compiled and compared by `just build-shaders --strict` and no DXIL is embedded.
