The water pictures' device time fell from 2.0-2.5 to 0.56 ms, and the frame's from 3.9-4.8 to
1.26-1.28 ms. `stage_submit_ms` fell by about 2.7 ms. `stage_build_ms` rose by about 0.7 ms,
because the processor now writes the dynamic streams across the bus once. Before, the device read
them across it twice. The mean frame fell from 13.6-13.7 to 11.4-11.7 ms.

after6 missed by 0.02 ms. Its worst frame, frame 10, had a `sky_ms` of 6.51 ms. Frame 10's
`sky_ms` in the other six takes was 4.21-4.40 ms, and every other band of that frame rose with
it. The README's budget section describes this shape: the process losing its processor, not one
band stalling. The water passes were 0.56 ms on that frame as on every other. The worst frames of
the other six takes were 13.8-15.6 ms. They are now set by the processor bands (`sky_ms`
4.2-4.8 ms, `stage_build_ms` and `stage_submit_ms` about 3 ms each), not by the device.
