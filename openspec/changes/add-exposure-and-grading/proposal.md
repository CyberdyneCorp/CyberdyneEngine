# Add physical exposure, auto-exposure and colour grading to the frame

## Why

`rendering-post-processing` asks for manual exposure in EV (or aperture, shutter and ISO) and
auto-exposure driven by a luminance histogram computed by compute shaders, with percentile target
selection, EV clamps, two adaptation speeds and a compensation curve; and for colour grading through
a 3D LUT applied in log space, with the parametric grade baked into that one table so the runtime
cost is one lookup, at step 12 of the chain — after tonemapping. The tree had the host arithmetic
(`post/exposure.h`, `post/grading.h`) but no device path: the frame's resolve multiplied by a number
of stops typed into the globals, nothing measured scene luminance, M11.c's own task list records
"Colour grading is NOT applied", and `requirements-coverage.toml` said of both requirements that the
runtime half was not claimed.

## What changes

- A new module, `src/rendering/grading/`: three compute dispatches (clear, 256-bin luminance
  histogram, adapt) that meter the frame's scene-referred colour before bloom and adapt an EV100
  toward the histogram's percentile mean; and a graded resolve — exposure, the same tone curve as
  `fullscreenResolve`, and one lookup into a baked 3D table — that a caller installs as the frame's
  `FramePassKind::PostProcess` callback. SPIR-V and MSL are committed and regenerated by
  `shaders/regenerate.py`; the vertex stage is the library's own `fullscreenVertex`.
- `post/lut.h`: a `.cube` parser, a display-referred log encoding with a linear toe
  (`log2(1 + 4096 x) / log2(4097)`), and `bake_display_lut`, which folds the parametric grade and a
  `.cube` look (read in its own sRGB or linear encoding) into one table indexed and stored in that
  encoding. `post/look.h`: the `.cygrade` look file. `post/exposure.h` gains the EV100-to-stops
  conversion, the histogram binning and the adaptation deadline the device uses.
- `samples/12-beauty` gains `--look <file.cygrade>`, two committed looks (warm from parameters,
  cool from a white balance and a `.cube`), and `just capture-beauty-grading`, which publishes the
  shot ungraded, warm and cool and fails unless the ungraded still is M11.c's pixels exactly.
- `post/exposure.h`'s `CameraExposure` and `exposure_multiplier(float)` are renamed
  `CameraControls` and `multiplier_for_ev100`. `lighting/units.h` defines both original names in the
  same namespace, so a source including both headers did not compile and a binary linking both
  modules' exposure objects did not link — latent until this change's suite linked the post
  metering beside the pipeline scene. `render.grading` carries the regression case.
- Tests: `unit.render_post` and `integration.render_post_bake` for the host arithmetic, and
  `render.grading` on a device.

## Scope

Exposure and grading only. The frame's own resolve, its shaders and every other stage are
untouched: a frame that does not install the graded resolve draws what it drew before. Not built,
and kept as exemptions with their reasons: a metering mask, exposure published to material shaders
for emissive units, the Custom tonemap curve, and HDR (PQ/scRGB) output through the graded resolve.
