# Add HDR bloom to the post chain

## Why

`rendering-post-processing` requires bloom produced by a progressive downsample and upsample chain,
with a soft threshold knee, a Karis average on the first downsample, a tent filter on upsample, an
artistic intensity, optional lens dirt and anamorphic stretch, and energy redistributed rather than
added. The tree had the arithmetic (`bloom_prefilter`, `karis_weight`) and the stage in the chain's
order, but no pass: `BloomSettings::mip_count` and `anamorphic` were read by nothing, lens dirt did
not exist, and `tools/roadmap/requirements-coverage.toml` recorded the requirement as `exempt:m11e`.

## What changes

- `forward/bloom_chain.h` declares the chain into the render graph: a prefilter, one downsample per
  level, one upsample per level back up, and a composite into a new full-resolution target. Each
  level is its own graph transient.
- `ForwardFrame` declares it as `FramePassKind::Bloom`, after the temporal stage and before the
  post-process, when `FrameFeatures::bloom` is set; the assembly sets it from
  `PostChainConfig::bloom`. The post-process reads `FrameResources::post_source`, which is the
  bloomed colour when bloom ran and exactly what it read before otherwise.
- `cy/bloom.slang` holds the four fragment entry points; `pipeline::BloomRenderer` records them over
  the frame's own full-screen vertex stage. `FrameRecorder::set_bloom` attaches it, and `bind()`
  refuses a frame whose chain has bloom and no renderer. The SPIR-V and MSL are committed and
  regenerated by `src/rendering/pipeline/shaders/embed_bloom.py`.
- The composite redistributes energy: `scene + intensity * (bloom - prefilter(scene))`, with level
  weights `(1 - s) s^k` that sum to one. `bloom_composite` in `post/effects.h` is the same
  expression on the processor, replacing a lerp that darkened every texel of the frame.
- `samples/12-beauty` gains `--bloom` and a bloom grade in `content/beauty/shot.cyshot`;
  `just capture-beauty-bloom` publishes the shot with and without bloom under `docs/design/images/`.

## Scope

Bloom only. Ambient occlusion is a parallel change in the same area and nothing here touches it.
D3D12 is not a target of this change: the fragment entry points compile to HLSL, and the vertex
stage is the frame's `fullscreenVertex`, whose `SV_VulkanVertexID` is the declared gap
`m11c:every-shader-reaches-every-target` closing at M11.d.
