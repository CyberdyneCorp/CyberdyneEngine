Two mutations were green at first and are recorded with what answered them:
  - `digest = hash_modifiers(...)` removed: 'a synchronised space has a phase of its own' stayed green,
    because the synchronised program also has one more parameter, which the digest already hashes.
    The case now also compiles a space with one sample moved — same code, clips and parameters —
    and requires a different digest; red after that.
  - the zero-weight gate in `modifier_input` removed: 'foot placement off is the frame before' stayed
    green, because `place_feet` and `solve_foot_placement` each refuse a zero weight as well. The
    mutation recorded above is the one that makes the pass-through itself inexact, which the case
    catches.

Not proven by a mutation: 'animation_system: planted feet are the same bytes from job workers as
from one thread'. One character's bytes can differ between threads only by a data race, which no
one-site mutation of this change introduces; the case pins the serial and parallel bytes equal.
