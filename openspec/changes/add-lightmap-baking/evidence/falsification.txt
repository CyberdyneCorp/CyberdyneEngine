Mutations that turn the lightmap suites red, Development build, Vulkan (RTX 5060). Each mutation
was applied, its suite rebuilt and run, the file restored and md5-verified; a mutation in
cy/frame.slang also regenerated the frame's SPIR-V and MSL, which were restored and verified with
it. Listed under each: the cases that failed.

Not provable on the device and proved on the host instead: the shading normal's response in the
directional and SH L1 decode. The device suite's corner has no normal-mapped surface, so at every
pixel the shading normal is the geometric one and the tilt term is zero there (a mutation dropping
`/ w` from the frame's directional decode survived). m03 holds the encoding's response in
integration.render_lightmap_bake, and render.lightmaps (b) holds the frame's term to the host's.
