The frame and the host GI with the device GI off. Development build, Vulkan on the RTX 5060.

THE FRAME. This slice changes no frame shader: `git diff origin/main --stat -- '*.slang'` lists only
src/rendering/gi_gpu/shaders/, which nothing in the forward frame compiles or binds, and
cy::rendering-gi-gpu and cy::rendering-raytracing-gpu are linked by render.gi_gpu and
render.gi_gpu_tracing alone. So the frame "with the feature off" is the frame main draws; the frame
suites pass unchanged (see the PR's test record).

THE HOST GI. What the slice changes in cy::rendering-gi is the radiance cache: `RadianceCache::select`
lifted out of `update()`, `ProbeGatherBackend`, `apply`, `collect`, a probe `revision`, and
`probe_ray_direction` (the anonymous `sphere_direction`, made public). With no backend installed the
host must compute exactly what main computed. That was checked by compiling one probe twice — once
against main's src/rendering/gi (git archive of origin/main) and once against this branch's — both
linked to the same core, raytracing and denoise libraries of build/dev, -O2:

  fourteen frames of IlluminationSystem::update over the host suites' room WITH its divider and the
  hardware tier on (the service traces the room's triangles), two probe levels at one metre, an
  invalidation at frame six and a camera moving 0.7 m a frame from frame eight, so probes are
  retired, created and reused; then 49 indirect_diffuse resolves over the floor; then, for each of
  the three encodings, a standalone RadianceCache over the same field and surface cache, scrolled
  0.5 m a frame from frame twelve, invalidated at frame nine, updated 24 times at a budget of 7
  probes, 12 rays a probe and an age cap of 5 frames. FNV-1a over every probe's position, level,
  axis distances, last update frame, error, importance, camera distance and flags, six gathers per
  probe, every update and placement report, and every resolve (the new `revision` deliberately not
  hashed: it is the one field main does not have).
