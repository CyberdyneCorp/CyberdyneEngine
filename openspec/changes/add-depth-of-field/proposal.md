# Gather depth of field from the physical circle of confusion

## Why

`rendering-post-processing` asks for depth of field "computed from a physically parameterised circle
of confusion derived from focal length, aperture, focus distance, and sensor size", gathered "with
separate near and far fields, a configurable bokeh shape … and correct occlusion handling so
near-field blur bleeds over in-focus geometry", at step 7 of the chain. The tree had the first half
only: `post/effects.h`'s `circle_of_confusion` is the signed thin-lens circle, tested, and nothing
gathered. `requirements-coverage.toml` recorded the requirement as exempt for exactly that reason —
"no near and far fields, no bokeh shape, no aperture texture, no occlusion handling".

## What changes

- The forward frame gains `FramePassKind::DepthOfField`, between the temporal resolve and bloom,
  declared by its producer through `FrameStageDeclaration`. `ScreenSpaceStageInputs` gains an
  appended `source` — the colour the chain has reached — and the frame creates
  `FrameResources::depth_of_field`, the full-resolution colour the stage writes and every later
  stage reads. Off by default and absent when off; refused without a producer, and refused on a
  multisampled frame without the prepass, whose depth resolve is the only single-sample depth.
  `FrameAssembly` maps `PostChainConfig::depth_of_field` to the stage and `FrameSinks::depth_of_field`
  to its producer.
- A new module, `src/rendering/depth_of_field/`: five compute dispatches — a half-resolution layer of
  colour and signed circle of confusion (the block's nearest surface), the near field's reach in
  tiles as wide as the largest radius and their 3x3 dilation, a scatter-as-gather of the far field
  (normalised, over the pixel's own radius, far texels only) and the near field (unnormalised: its
  sum is the pixel's coverage by near-field discs), and a full-resolution composite with a
  bilateral far upsample. The radius in pixels is half `circle_of_confusion` times the image height;
  `focus.h` holds the host twin of every shader expression. A circular aperture or 5–16 straight
  blades. SPIR-V and MSL committed, regenerated by `shaders/regenerate.py`.
- `samples/12-beauty` gains `--depth-of-field <target>` and two focus targets in the shot file,
  and `just capture-beauty-depth-of-field` publishes the shot focused on the copper sphere and on a
  far column, failing unless the frame without the stage is M11.c's pixels exactly.
- The frame recorder's temporal resolve and post-process, and the beauty sample's resolve, bind
  their graphics pipeline before their descriptor sets: the backend binds sets at the last bound
  pipeline's point, and the stage's compute composite is the first compute work right before the
  post-process.
- Tests: `unit.render_dof` (the twins against the lens and the projection), `unit.render_forward`
  (the stage's place, its inputs and its refusals) and `render.depth_of_field` on a device.

## Scope

Depth of field only. No existing shader changes: a frame that does not switch the stage on declares
what it declared before. Not built, and kept in the coverage note: a custom aperture texture (the
blades are the configurable shape this change ships), a temporal accumulation of the gather, and
occlusion of the far field by nearer far-field layers (the far field is gathered as one layer).
Autofocus is `track_focus`, already tested; the sample focuses on a named point instead.
