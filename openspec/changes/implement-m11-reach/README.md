# implement-m11-reach

**M11 of the roadmap, the 1.0 gate — and superseded by the five rungs it was split into.**

Opened unscoped by M10's closing gate (task 8.5) so the ladder continued as a deliberate act. Its
first task was not code: *is M11 one milestone or two?* The answer is **five**, and the work lives in
`implement-m11a-foundations`, `implement-m11b-authoring`, `implement-m11c-image`,
`implement-m11d-desktop` and `implement-m11e-ship`.

**This change is kept rather than deleted or folded into a rung.** `proposal.md` is the record of
what M11 inherited — seven declared gaps that run and fail, a world costing seven times its frame
budget, `save-and-persistence` mis-scoped rather than late — and of the argument that produced the
seam. `tasks.md` records the decisions as taken and names the rung that owns each row.
`specs/delivery-roadmap/` carries the two rules the split had to add: how a handover criterion works
once several rungs are opened at once, and that a superseded milestone change is kept as the record
of its own split.
