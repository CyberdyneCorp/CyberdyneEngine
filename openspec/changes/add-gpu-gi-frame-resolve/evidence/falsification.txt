Mutations of the stage 4 and 5 code, Development build, Vulkan on the RTX 5060 (driver 580.95.05).
Each was applied to the source named, the gi_gpu SPIR-V/MSL (or the frame's, for cy/frame.slang)
regenerated through the module's regenerate.py where the mutation is in a shader, the named targets
rebuilt and the named cases run, then the source and both headers restored and md5-verified. RED
means the case failed with the mutation in place.

Four survived the first pass and the suites were strengthened rather than the mutations dropped:

  m3 (the accumulation's floor on its blend weight) survived an 8-frame comparison, because the floor
  is reached only past the signal's history length; the software-tier comparison runs 40 frames now.
  m4 (the upsample's normal power) survived on a room whose neighbouring normals agree exactly; the
  half-grid comparison ripples every normal by a few degrees now.
  m6 and m7 (the composite's plain-surface branch, and the plain-surface admission bit) survived
  because the specular term alone changed the picture; (b) now also requires the diffuse alone to.
  m8's first form (returning a constant) did not compile (an unused parameter under -Werror) and
  was rewritten as a threshold no scale reaches.

The records below are the reruns. Two greens are listed because they are expected rather than gaps:
m9's arbiter-report case reports a cost and a rung and asserts no relaxation, which m9 does not touch;
m10's device comparison holds the camera still, so no pixel's history is depth-rejected on either
side — the host case that moves the camera is the one that sees the tolerance.
