THE FIRST OFF BUILD WAS NOT IDENTICAL, AND WHY. With the cloud pass choosing its clear sky by a
select (`atmosphereSky > 0.5 ? loaded : standInClear(direction)`), 3 of 192 frames differed from
before in one or two pixels by one 8-bit step (frames 98, 102, 103, dusk sky). The base build is
deterministic — two takes of it compare 192 of 192 — and restoring only the committed
world_visual_spirv.h made the off take identical again, so the difference was the recompiled cloud
pass. With a branch instead of a select, the stand-in path is the arithmetic it was, and the off take
is identical frame for frame (above).
