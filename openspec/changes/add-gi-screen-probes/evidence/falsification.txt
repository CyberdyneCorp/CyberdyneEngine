Mutations of the screen-probe gather, Development build, Vulkan on the RTX 5060 (driver 580.95.05),
run against commit 5fec1810. Each was applied to the source named — for a shader, the gi_gpu SPIR-V
and MSL regenerated through the module's regenerate.py — `just build-engine` rebuilt the tree, the
named cases ran, and the source and both headers were restored and md5-verified. RED means the case
failed with the mutation in place.

Five survived an earlier pass and the suites were strengthened rather than the mutations dropped:

  h4 and d2 (the blend's count weight, 1/(count+2)) survived because the floor, 1/history_frames,
  masked the count's weight at the history lengths the cases used; the blend case keeps eight
  frames and the device comparison four now, so the count's own weight decides.
  h9 (the integration skipping adaptive probes) survived a case that measured every face, where
  uniform probes stood on the block's sides too; the case now measures the pixels no uniform
  probe within a tile stands for.
  d1 (the device's normal weight to the fourth power, not the eighth) survived on the axis-aligned
  room, where every normal weight is one or zero; a placement case on normals rippled by up to
  about five degrees catches it. (The probes' estimates are not compared on rippled normals: a
  normal turned by its last bit turns a grazing ray to the other side of an edge.)
  d7 (the device lever ignored) survived (e)'s "the two frames differ", which two runs of one
  gather also satisfy; (e) now reads back the probes' frame's records.

A sixth, the renormalisation of a probe's estimate by its answered rays (a back face with no cache
to stand in), survived the host quality suite: with ray origins moved clear of other surfaces it
moved the room's error by 0.001. It was taken out of both sides rather than kept untested.
